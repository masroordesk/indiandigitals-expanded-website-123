Local SEO & Google Business Profile FAQs

Is Google Business Profile management the same as local SEO?

It is the core part of it. Local SEO also covers citations, location pages, reviews and on-site signals that support your profile.

Can you manage multiple locations?

Yes. We manage profiles, location pages and reporting across branches while keeping data consistent.

How do you handle reviews?

We set up simple review request flows and draft professional replies. We never buy or fake reviews.

What is the difference between Google Business Profile and GMB?

They are the same thing. Google renamed Google My Business to Google Business Profile.

Can I rank without a shop address?

Service-area businesses can rank, though results depend on the area you serve and your competitors.

How many reviews do I need?

There is no magic number. Steady, recent, genuine reviews matter more than a one-time burst.

Do you work on listings outside Google?

Yes. We clean up Bing, Apple Maps and major directories as part of citation work.

How long does it take to see results from local seo & google business profile?

Technical fixes and quick wins often show movement within 60 to 90 days. Compounding results such as authority, pipeline and AI-search visibility usually take 4 to 6 months of steady execution. We share a plain-language weekly update so you always know what changed.

How much does it cost and how do we start?

Pricing depends on scope, competition and the channels involved, so we scope each engagement after a free 30-minute discovery call. Message us through the contact form or WhatsApp and we will review your current digital footprint first.

Can I combine local seo & google business profile with other services?

Yes. It pairs especially well with Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Social Media Marketing and Paid Media Management, and we plan them under one roadmap so work builds on itself.

Do you offer local seo & google business profile for businesses in my city?
