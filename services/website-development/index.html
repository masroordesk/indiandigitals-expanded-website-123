Website Development

Website development that loads fast and converts

We build marketing websites on modern stacks, whether hand-coded, AI-assisted (vibe coded) or on a CMS, whichever fits your goals and budget.

Start a Conversation See Our Services

Who website development is for

Businesses with a slow, dated or hard-to-edit website, startups launching their first site, and brands moving to a faster stack.

Headless frameworks, modern static site generation and CMS platforms built for speed, clean architecture, responsive layouts and conversion paths.

We design the site around how buyers decide, then pick the lightest stack that gets the job done. Speed, crawlability and clear calls to action are built in from the first line of code.

What's included in Website Development

  • Headless and static-site builds (Next.js, Astro, plain HTML)
  • CMS builds on WordPress, Webflow and similar platforms
  • Vibe-coded and AI-assisted builds, reviewed by a human
  • Core Web Vitals and page-speed engineering
  • Schema-ready markup and clean URL architecture
  • Conversion paths, forms and analytics wiring

Every engagement is scoped around your goals, your market and the team you already have. We document what we do each week so you can see exactly what changed and why it matters.

We would rather do fewer things properly than stretch across too many. That is why scopes start with the highest-impact work and expand only when results justify it.

What we measure

We report on outcomes tied to the business, not vanity numbers.

Page speed (LCP)

How quickly the main content appears on a mobile connection.

Enquiry conversion rate

The share of visitors who send a form, call or message.

Index coverage

Common mistakes we fix

Fix: Use lean code and load only what each page needs.

No redirect plan

Fix: Map every old URL to a new one before launch.

Forms that go nowhere

Fix: Test every form and send enquiries to an inbox someone watches.

Website Development FAQs

Do you build with vibe coding or traditional development?

Both. We choose the approach per project. AI-assisted builds can be faster and cheaper for simple sites, and we review the output for performance, accessibility and SEO before launch.

Which platforms do you work with?

Static and headless frameworks, WordPress, Webflow and custom HTML hosted on Vercel or Cloudflare. We recommend what is simplest for your team to maintain.

Will the site be SEO-ready at launch?

Yes. Titles, meta descriptions, schema, internal links, sitemaps and redirects are handled as part of the build.

How long does a website take to build?

A focused marketing site usually takes 3 to 6 weeks. Larger sites with many pages or custom features take longer.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. We choose a setup your team can update, and we hand over a short guide.

Do you handle hosting and domain setup?

We deploy to fast hosts such as Vercel or Cloudflare and help connect your domain.

Will I lose rankings when the site changes?

Not if redirects, titles and content are carried over carefully. We check this before and after launch.

How long does it take to see results from website development?

Technical fixes and quick wins often show movement within 60 to 90 days. Compounding results such as authority, pipeline and AI-search visibility usually take 4 to 6 months of steady execution. We share a plain-language weekly update so you always know what changed.

How much does it cost and how do we start?

Pricing depends on scope, competition and the channels involved, so we scope each engagement after a free 30-minute discovery call. Message us through the contact form or WhatsApp and we will review your current digital footprint first.

Can I combine website development with other services?

Yes. It pairs especially well with Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Graphic Design & Creative Production and Complete Digital Marketing Management, and we plan them under one roadmap so work builds on itself.

Do you offer website development for businesses in my city?

Yes. We work from Noida, Varanasi, Ahmedabad and Prayagraj and serve clients across India, the USA, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Africa. See our locations page for details.
