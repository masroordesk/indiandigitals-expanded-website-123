LinkedIn Strategy & Executive Ghostwriting FAQs

Will the posts sound like me?

That is the goal. We work from your own words, stories and opinions and you approve every post.

How much time does it need from us?

Usually one short call a week or two, plus quick approvals.

Can you manage company pages as well?

Yes, we can run the company page and set up employee advocacy alongside personal profiles.

Do you post on my behalf?

Only with your approval. You can also post yourself using our drafts.

How do you keep the voice authentic?

We work from your own words in interviews and edit until it sounds like you.

Can you also help the company page?

Yes, and we can set up simple employee advocacy so the team shares key posts.

How long until it works?

Most profiles see better engagement in 4 to 8 weeks and more inbound conversations in 3 to 4 months.

How long does it take to see results from linkedin strategy & executive ghostwriting?

Technical fixes and quick wins often show movement within 60 to 90 days. Compounding results such as authority, pipeline and AI-search visibility usually take 4 to 6 months of steady execution. We share a plain-language weekly update so you always know what changed.

How much does it cost and how do we start?

Pricing depends on scope, competition and the channels involved, so we scope each engagement after a free 30-minute discovery call. Message us through the contact form or WhatsApp and we will review your current digital footprint first.

Can I combine linkedin strategy & executive ghostwriting with other services?

Yes. It pairs especially well with B2B Business Writing & Sales Enablement, Content Strategy & Editorial Planning and Social Media Marketing, and we plan them under one roadmap so work builds on itself.

Do you offer linkedin strategy & executive ghostwriting for businesses in my city?
