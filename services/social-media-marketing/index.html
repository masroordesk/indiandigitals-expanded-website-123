Social Media Marketing FAQs

Which platforms do you cover?

LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube and Pinterest, depending on your audience.

Do you create the visuals too?

Yes. Our graphic design team produces the creatives so the content looks consistent.

Can social media help SEO?

Indirectly. It builds brand searches, distributes content and earns mentions that support search and AI visibility.

How often should we post?

Three to five quality posts a week is a good start for most brands. Consistency beats volume.

Do you run paid social too?

Yes, through our paid media service, so organic and ads support each other.

Can you handle comments and DMs?

We can manage community replies, and flag sales conversations to your team.

Do you create videos?

We plan and edit short videos and can work with footage you provide.

How long does it take to see results from social media marketing?

Technical fixes and quick wins often show movement within 60 to 90 days. Compounding results such as authority, pipeline and AI-search visibility usually take 4 to 6 months of steady execution. We share a plain-language weekly update so you always know what changed.

How much does it cost and how do we start?

Pricing depends on scope, competition and the channels involved, so we scope each engagement after a free 30-minute discovery call. Message us through the contact form or WhatsApp and we will review your current digital footprint first.

Can I combine social media marketing with other services?

Yes. It pairs especially well with Graphic Design & Creative Production, LinkedIn Strategy & Executive Ghostwriting and Content Writing & Blog Production, and we plan them under one roadmap so work builds on itself.

Do you offer social media marketing for businesses in my city?

Yes. We work from Noida, Varanasi, Ahmedabad and Prayagraj and serve clients across India, the USA, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Africa. See our locations page for details.
