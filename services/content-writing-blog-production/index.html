Content Writing & Blog Production FAQs

Do you use AI to write?

We use tools for research and structure but every piece is written and edited by a person for accuracy and voice.

Can you publish directly to our blog?

Yes. We can publish and optimize posts on your CMS.

How long are the articles?

As long as the topic needs. Most range from 1,200 to 2,500 words.

Do you provide images and graphics?

Yes. We can add charts, diagrams and simple custom visuals.

How many articles can you deliver monthly?

Typically 4 to 12, depending on depth and subject expertise needed.

Will the content pass AI detectors?

We write and edit by hand, but we focus on quality and accuracy rather than detector scores.

Can you write in our brand voice?

Yes. We start with samples and a short style guide.

How long does it take to see results from content writing & blog production?

Technical fixes and quick wins often show movement within 60 to 90 days. Compounding results such as authority, pipeline and AI-search visibility usually take 4 to 6 months of steady execution. We share a plain-language weekly update so you always know what changed.

How much does it cost and how do we start?

Pricing depends on scope, competition and the channels involved, so we scope each engagement after a free 30-minute discovery call. Message us through the contact form or WhatsApp and we will review your current digital footprint first.

Can I combine content writing & blog production with other services?

Yes. It pairs especially well with Content Strategy & Editorial Planning, Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and B2B Business Writing & Sales Enablement, and we plan them under one roadmap so work builds on itself.

Do you offer content writing & blog production for businesses in my city?

Yes. We work from Noida, Varanasi, Ahmedabad and Prayagraj and serve clients across India, the USA, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Africa. See our locations page for details.
