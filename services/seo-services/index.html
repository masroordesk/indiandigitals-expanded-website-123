Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

SEO that earns non-branded visibility

We fix the technical foundation, build topical authority and target searches your buyers actually make, so organic traffic turns into enquiries.

Who search engine optimization (seo) is for

Companies that depend on organic enquiries, sites that have stopped growing, and teams that need a clear priority list instead of a long audit PDF.

Core technical health, crawl architecture, topical authority clusters, schema markup and non-branded search visibility.

We start with a technical and competitive audit, then build a roadmap that sequences quick fixes ahead of long-term authority work. Every task ties to a commercial outcome, not a vanity metric.

What's included in Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

  • Technical audits and crawl-budget clean-up
  • Site architecture and internal linking
  • Topical authority clusters and keyword mapping
  • Schema markup and structured data
  • On-page optimization for commercial intent
  • Monthly ranking, traffic and lead reporting

Every engagement is scoped around your goals, your market and the team you already have. We document what we do each week so you can see exactly what changed and why it matters.

We would rather do fewer things properly than stretch across too many. That is why scopes start with the highest-impact work and expand only when results justify it.

What we measure

We report on outcomes tied to the business, not vanity numbers.

Non-branded clicks

Visits from searches that do not include your company name.

Ranking pages for money keywords

How many commercial pages sit in the top 10.

Organic enquiries

Leads that came from search, tracked in your forms and calls.

Common mistakes we fix

Chasing high-volume keywords

Fix: Target searches that show buying intent, even if volume is lower.

Thin, duplicate pages

Fix: Give each page one clear purpose and unique content.

Ignoring internal links

Fix: Link related pages so authority and visitors flow to your key pages.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) FAQs

What makes your SEO different?

We optimize for qualified enquiries, not raw traffic. That means commercial-intent keywords, strong page structure and clean technical health.

Do you do link building?

We focus on earned, relevant mentions and citations rather than low-quality link packages, which also supports AI-search visibility.

Can you work with our existing website?

Yes. We audit your current site first and improve it in place, or recommend a rebuild only if the platform is the blocker.

How much content do we need?

Enough to cover each core topic well. We usually start with the 10 to 20 pages most tied to revenue.

Do you need access to our website?

Yes, to Search Console, analytics and the CMS, so we can make or guide changes.

What if our site is on WordPress, Webflow or custom code?

We work with all of them and adapt recommendations to what the platform allows.

How do you report progress?

A plain weekly note on what changed and a monthly summary of rankings, traffic and leads.

How long does it take to see results from search engine optimization (seo)?

Technical fixes and quick wins often show movement within 60 to 90 days. Compounding results such as authority, pipeline and AI-search visibility usually take 4 to 6 months of steady execution. We share a plain-language weekly update so you always know what changed.

How much does it cost and how do we start?

Pricing depends on scope, competition and the channels involved, so we scope each engagement after a free 30-minute discovery call. Message us through the contact form or WhatsApp and we will review your current digital footprint first.

Can I combine search engine optimization (seo) with other services?

Yes. It pairs especially well with AI Search Optimization (GEO, AEO, LLMO), Content Strategy & Editorial Planning and Local SEO & Google Business Profile, and we plan them under one roadmap so work builds on itself.

Do you offer search engine optimization (seo) for businesses in my city?

Yes. We work from Noida, Varanasi, Ahmedabad and Prayagraj and serve clients across India, the USA, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Africa. See our locations page for details.
