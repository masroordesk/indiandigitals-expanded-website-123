Who paid media management is for

Paid Media Management FAQs

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads and Meta are our core. We can add other platforms when they suit your audience.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

Fees are agreed in the proposal based on scope. We keep them transparent so you know exactly what you pay for.

How soon will ads generate leads?

Campaigns can produce data in the first few weeks. Stable cost per lead usually takes 2 to 3 months of optimization.

What budget do I need to start?

Enough to gather useful data. We recommend a test budget after reviewing your market and goals.

Do you manage LinkedIn Ads for B2B?

Yes. LinkedIn costs more per click but can reach exact job titles and companies.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. We work inside your accounts so you keep full control and history.

How often will I see reports?

Weekly for spend and leads, with a monthly review of what to change.

How long does it take to see results from paid media management?

Technical fixes and quick wins often show movement within 60 to 90 days. Compounding results such as authority, pipeline and AI-search visibility usually take 4 to 6 months of steady execution. We share a plain-language weekly update so you always know what changed.

How much does it cost and how do we start?

Pricing depends on scope, competition and the channels involved, so we scope each engagement after a free 30-minute discovery call. Message us through the contact form or WhatsApp and we will review your current digital footprint first.

Can I combine paid media management with other services?

Yes. It pairs especially well with Email Marketing & Lifecycle Automation, Graphic Design & Creative Production and Complete Digital Marketing Management, and we plan them under one roadmap so work builds on itself.

Do you offer paid media management for businesses in my city?

Yes. We work from Noida, Varanasi, Ahmedabad and Prayagraj and serve clients across India, the USA, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Africa. See our locations page for details.
