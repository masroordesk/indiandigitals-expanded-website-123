What services does Indian Digitals offer?

We provide full-funnel digital marketing: SEO, local SEO, AI search (GEO/AEO/LLMO), paid media, social media, LinkedIn strategy, content strategy and writing, B2B business writing, email marketing, graphic design, website development and complete digital marketing management.

Do you work with B2B businesses?

Yes. A large share of our work is for B2B companies, manufacturers, professional services and SaaS providers with long, multi-stakeholder buying cycles.

Do you work with local businesses?

Yes. We handle local SEO and Google Business Profile management for local brands, clinics and multi-location businesses.

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO, also called GEO, AEO or LLMO, is the work of structuring your content, entity data and citations so ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews mention and recommend your business.

What is the difference between SEO and AI SEO?

Traditional SEO earns rankings in search results. AI SEO earns mentions and citations inside AI-generated answers. They support each other, so we run both.

Do you provide website development?

Yes. We build fast, responsive, SEO-ready websites on modern static, headless and CMS platforms, including AI-assisted builds.

How long does SEO take to produce results?

Early movement often appears in 60 to 90 days. Durable authority and AI-search presence typically take 4 to 6 months.
