Why direct review links matter

What is a Google Review Link and QR Code Generator?

When customers have to search for your business name on Google, open your profile, scroll to the reviews tab, and tap write a review, most of them give up halfway. A direct Google review link removes those extra steps.

Our free generator builds a direct URL and encodes it into a high-contrast QR code. When a customer scans the code with their iPhone or Android camera, their browser opens your Google Business Profile with the 5-star rating window ready to submit.

Because the QR code encodes your permanent Google link directly, it never expires and never shows third-party ads or redirect screens.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my Google Place ID or review link?

Sign in to the Google account that manages your Google Business Profile. Search your business name on Google, click Ask for reviews in your profile dashboard, and copy the short g.page link. If you have a Google Place ID instead, switch to the Place ID tab above and paste it in to create the official review link automatically.

Do these Google review QR codes expire or have a scan limit?

No. This tool creates static QR codes that store your review URL directly inside the pattern. There are no middleman redirects, no expiration dates, and no scan limits.

Does the QR code open the 5-star review box directly?

Yes. Scanning the code takes the user straight to your Google Business Profile review form so they can tap the stars and write a comment immediately, provided they are logged into a Google account on their phone.

Is it safe to add my business logo inside the QR code?

Yes. The generator sets error correction to Level H (high), which allows up to 30% of the QR pattern to be covered by your logo while remaining easy to scan. Always test the final code with your phone camera before printing in bulk.

Should I download PNG or SVG?

Use PNG for websites, social media, WhatsApp, email signatures, and standard office printing. Use SVG if you are sending the QR code to a designer or print shop for large banners, glass decals, or packaging, because SVG stays sharp at any size.

How do Google reviews improve local SEO rankings?

Google evaluates review quantity, how recently reviews were posted, and your overall star rating when ranking local businesses. Earning steady reviews helps your business rank higher in Google Maps and local search results.
