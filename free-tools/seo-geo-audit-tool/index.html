Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is an SEO and GEO audit?

An SEO audit reviews how well your webpage is structured for search engines like Google, checking title tags, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, and mobile load speed. A GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) audit checks whether AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can clearly understand your brand entity, schema markup, and structured answers.

Is this SEO and GEO audit tool free?

Yes. Every visitor gets 5 free website audits per day directly in the browser with no sign-up or credit card required.

Why do some websites fail to load in Live URL mode?

Certain websites use strict firewall rules that block automated readers from downloading their HTML. If a site blocks live fetching, switch to the Paste Page HTML tab, copy the page source from your browser, and paste it in to run the full analysis.

How does JSON-LD schema help my website rank in AI search?

Structured data gives search crawlers and large language models explicit, verified facts about your business, services, authors, and FAQs. Pages with clear Organization, Service, and FAQPage schema are easier for AI engines to trust and cite.

How can I fix the issues found in my audit report?

Start with the items marked Fail in the Prioritized Action Items list at the top of your report. Fixing missing title tags, duplicate H1s, missing schema markup, and slow mobile load times usually delivers the fastest ranking gains.
